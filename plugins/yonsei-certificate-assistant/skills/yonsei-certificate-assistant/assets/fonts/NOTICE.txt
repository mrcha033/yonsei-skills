Yonsei University font notice

This directory contains the Yonsei title and body fonts used to preserve the
original appearance of issued certificate PDFs:

- 연세제목.TTF (PostScript name: YonseiB)
- 연세본문.TTF (PostScript name: YonseiL)

The project publisher reports having received permission from a Yonsei
University professor to redistribute these font files for this student tool.
The fonts retain their original copyright and are not covered by this
repository's MIT License. Do not treat the MIT License as a separate license
to extract, modify, or redistribute the font files outside that permission.

Released copies are pinned by SHA-256 in the certificate assistant. If a font
is missing or changed, certificate rendering stops instead of substituting a
different font.
