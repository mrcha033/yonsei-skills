# Official graduation sources

Use the exact admission year, campus, college, department, major combination.
Graduation rules can differ even when the major name is the same.

Start with:

- Yonsei University academic catalog and regulations:
  `https://www.yonsei.ac.kr/sc/389/subview.do`
- 2026 university catalog PDF:
  `https://www.yonsei.ac.kr/bbs/sc/61/1012667/download.do`
- 2026 curricula PDF:
  `https://www.yonsei.ac.kr/bbs/sc/61/1013218/download.do`

Then locate the official department page for the student's exact program.
Record the checked date and the section or table used. If the catalog and
department page conflict, do not choose silently: show both and direct the
student to the department office or official portal graduation audit.

Do not treat an unofficial community post, remembered rule, or another
student's calculation as the requirement source.
